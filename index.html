The case file

Ten districts. One night shift.

The Kingpin's crews run the rats, the cats and the crows. They took the parks, then the streets, then the people who looked out for them. Barnaby fixes the gear, Brindle works the radio, Pip knows every shortcut. You do the rest.

How it plays

Built like the arcade brawlers

A 2.5D belt-scroller with four layers of parallax, arena lock-ins that open into full 3D rooms, and a combat system deep enough to style on the Syndicate.

Street brawling

Jab, cross, roundhouse and tailspin combos. Counter the red “!” telegraph, juggle into the air and finish them off against a wall or a wrecked car.

Arena lock-ins

The camera pushes into a 3D room and the doors slam shut. Clear the waves, use the props and keep moving.

Grapple & glide

Yank thugs to you, zip to the heavies, rip weapons out of their hands, ride car roofs, then spread your arms and dive-bomb from above.

Street Cred

Pipes, claws, chains, crowbars. Mix up your moves to push your rank from Dirty to Awesome, then spend your acorns in the Stash.

The case board

From the back alleys to the penthouse

Ten hand-painted districts, each with its own crew and a boss who doesn't go down easy.

Moveset

Fight dirty

  • CounterWhen a red “!” pops over a thug, hit heavy to reverse the swing and strip his weapon.
  • GrapplePull small fry in, zip yourself to big ones for a dropkick, or snag a weapon off the floor.
  • DisarmWeapons have durability. Steal them, swing them, then throw what's left.
  • Flying SquirrelSmash a ★ crate for a Golden Acorn. Then hold jump to glide, ride the vents, and press down + attack to dive-bomb.
  • Bio-SurgeFill the meter, then hit special to clear the screen.
